## 🎬 **Spectator Mode Enhancements**

### **🎙️ Commentary System (NEW IDEA - HIGH PRIORITY)**
**Concept**: In spectator mode, have 2 AI masters playing the game while the user + another AI master profile act as commentators

**Implementation Ideas:**
- **4-Way Dynamic**: Two players (e.g., Kasparov vs Tal) + Two commentators (User + Fischer)
- **Hearing Options**: 
  - Players can't hear commentators (clean gameplay)
  - Players occasionally overhear commentary (emergent reactions!)
  - Full transparency mode (maximum chaos and interaction)
- **Commentary Triggers**:
  - Brilliant moves get excited commentary
  - Blunders trigger criticism/sympathy
  - Time pressure creates tension
  - Commentators disagree and debate among themselves
- **Emergent Behavior Opportunities**:
  - Players react to overheard commentary with emotional responses
  - Commentators develop chemistry and inside jokes
  - Players adjust style based on commentary feedback
  - Meta-commentary about the commentary system itself

**Technical Implementation:**
- Extend `SpectatorConversationOrchestrator` for 4-way dynamics
- Add commentary emotional triggers separate from player emotions
- Create eavesdropping probability system for players
- Enhanced conversation branching for commentary vs. gameplay discussion

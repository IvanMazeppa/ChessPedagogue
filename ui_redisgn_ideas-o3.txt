## What’s Working

### 1. Depth & Layering

* Multi-panel frosted cards separate features neatly. This layered approach mirrors top-tier templates on Dribbble, where multiple glass surfaces create a tactile feel ([dribbble.com][4]).
* Rounded corners and soft shadows on buttons feel appropriately friendly and modern ([dribbble.com][5]).

### 2. Color Harmony

* The purple-blue gradient gives a futuristic vibe. High-quality glassmorphism UIs often pair a deep background with lighter, semi-transparent foreground elements for contrast ([dribbble.com][6]).
* Your electric-blue buttons pop against the deeper backdrop—just watch for over-saturation when layering blurs ([dribbble.com][2]).

## Where to Push Further

### 1. Refine Your Blur & Opacity

* Use a **backdrop-filter: blur(20px)** style (or Android Compose equivalent) for main panels, but keep inner overlays around **10 dp** max so background shapes peek through without washing out ([dribbble.com][1]).
* Target **15–25% opacity** on frosted cards; if you go above 30%, details behind it disappear too much ([dribbble.com][2]).

### 2. Boost Contrast & Legibility

* For text like “Battle Progress” and move list, add a **0.5 dp tinted border** behind the text or a slightly darker text shadow to anchor it ([reddit.com][3]).
* On smaller labels/buttons, bump font weight or switch to a white-on-deep-blue theme to match best practices in chess apps like Deep Green’s minimalist UI, which uses clean piece art and strong type for clarity ([wired.com][7]).

### 3. Elevate Iconography & Typography

* Consider custom chess-piece icons with slight inner glow to reinforce the AI theme, similar to Google’s GenChess which blends generative-AI motifs into piece art ([theverge.com][8]).
* Adopt a two-tier typography scale: large weight for scores (“Player vs Alekhine”), medium for buttons, small but bold for moves, just like top apps in WIRED’s “Best Chess Apps” roundup ([wired.com][9]).

## Next-Step Ideas

### 1. Dynamic Backdrop Blur

Animate blur radius slightly when switching panels or opening settings to give a living, responsive feel.

### 2. Subtle Micro-Interactions

Add a quick scale/opacity pulse on button tap or when a move is registered—small touches that delight.

### 3. Theme & Customization

Offer light/dark and “Aurora” (purple/teal) themes, letting players pick their glassmorphism flavor.
